# Phase 0B semantic evidence — Order and PharmacyBid schemas

**Baseline:** `main @ 22526bedb77a3d8148219036367e4714f401aecc`

**Members read in full:** `src/schemas/order.schema.ts:1–151`

The file defines embedded `OrderResult`, `OrderItem` and `StateTransition` schemas, an `Order` collection, and `PharmacyBid` (`7–45,47–126,128–149`). Order identity is a generated string UUID with required patient ID, optional pharmacy/prescription IDs, item list, subtotal/delivery/total defaults, enum-backed state/history/timeline, delivery mode/address, rejection/escalation, payment fields, finance fields, geo-dispatch object, split-order links, basket-review fields, transaction ID, insurance preauthorization fields and medical results (`47–121`). Indexes exist for patient chronology and pharmacy/state (`124–126`). PharmacyBid has generated ID, prescription/request and pharmacy IDs, items, required total/expiry and a four-value status enum (`128–149`).

The schema does not derive or validate `subtotal`, `delivery_fee`, `total`, discounts, wallet/loyalty amounts, cancellation fee or insurance copay; many money/payment fields are untyped numbers/strings with no currency, precision, non-negative, tax, server-provenance, ledger or state consistency constraints (`56–58,76–90,118`). Defaults such as cash/pending and zero amounts may conflate incomplete provisioning with real business state. `payment_status`, `refund_status` and `paid_via` are free strings, while transaction/refund timestamps are optional (`76–90`).

Order items allow manual/unverified entries, substitutions, unavailable flags and free-text rejection reasons without catalogue, dosage, pricing, quantity, unit, substitution equivalence or clinical validation (`18–33`). Patient name/phone, delivery address/coordinates, notes, insurance member/card image and medical results are persisted without an explicit minimization/retention/redaction policy in this member (`50–54,69–75,112–121`). Coordinates and address have no bounds/format validation. State history/timeline contain actor strings but no schema-level transition allowlist, monotonicity, version or audit authenticity (`36–45,61–66`).

Geo dispatch and basket review are open object/enum structures with no atomicity, candidate authorization, distance validation, state-transition constraints or timeout lifecycle (`91–110`). Split-order parent/sub-order relationships lack referential/acyclic/unique constraints (`99–101`). Insurance fields lack provider/member/card validation, secure asset policy, decision actor and amount reconciliation (`111–119`). PharmacyBid lacks compound uniqueness, bid ownership validation, currency/amount validation, expiration enforcement, decision actor/time and accepted-bid exclusivity (`128–149`). No code was changed and no build/test/database operation was performed during this semantic read.
