Barcode has no format/check-digit/global product authority validation (`38–39`). Images/image slots/SEO descriptions are raw strings with no media ACL, signed access, content/size/malware/retention or visible-content consistency controls (`16,56,79–86`).

No idempotency, atomic catalog/inventory update, audit/notification, search-index consistency or live inventory/coverage/review runtime evidence is established by this source read.
