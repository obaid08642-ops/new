# Phase 0B semantic evidence — Radiology schemas

**Baseline:** `main @ 22526bedb77a3d8148219036367e4714f401aecc`

**Member read in full:** `src/schemas/radiology.schema.ts:1–207`

The file defines `RadiologyService`, `RadiologyMachine`, the `RadiologyBookingState` enum and transition map, and `RadiologyBooking` (`1–207`). Catalog services contain bilingual names/descriptions, modality/body part, price/old price, preparation and referral requirements, availability/delivery capabilities, popularity, active/deleted/public/indexing flags, medical-review status, provenance/version, images, duration, safety-risk flags, cash/insurance availability and special notes (`10–67`). A text index exists on bilingual names and a modality/popularity index (`65–67`). Machines have provider_id/name/type/active and a provider/active index (`69–78`).

The booking state enum covers pending/new request, insurance/coplay, confirmed, arrival, scanning, report draft/review/ready/published, abort and cancelled; the transition map is explicit for most transitions, including abort recovery to confirmed/cancelled (`80–109`). Booking fields include patient identity/contact, any-typed items, total and several fee/price fields, home/facility location/address, scheduled time/instructions, referral object, state/history/reports, technician/provider references, payment and insurance details, documents, medical justification, machine and stored report/media links, delivery mode, scan identity, abort/report workflow, preparation, safety questionnaire, timestamps, rescheduling/insurance fields and referring-doctor notification (`111–207`). Indexes cover patient-createdAt and state-scheduled_at (`205–207`).

Critical gaps: catalog modality/body_part, prices, old_price, turnaround, duration, availability flags, public/indexing flags and review/provenance are not backed by comprehensive numeric, enum, currency, capability or approval invariants (`12–64`). `public_eligibility` and `indexing_eligibility` are separate booleans with no coupled publication state, and provenance/version/review timestamps do not prove who approved or what source was used (`37–44`). Service-level referral/home-visit/pregnancy/metal/contrast safety flags can conflict without cross-field validation (`23–32,48–60`).

`RadiologyBooking.items`, `state_history`, `reports`, `documents`, address, referral and safety questionnaire use `any`/Object-shaped structures or unconstrained nested objects; item service identity/name/modality/body_part/price is not schema-bound to a catalog snapshot (`118,131–142,182–189`). `total`, service/home/transport fees, total_price, copay and insurance fields have no nonnegative/decimal/currency/authority/reconciliation rules, allowing contradictory financial representations (`119–124,137–141,198–199`). No atomic server-side recalculation or payment/insurance state invariant is declared. Location and schedule have no coordinate/range/timezone/window validation (`126–130`).

Patient name/phone, address, instructions, notes, clinical impressions, referral base64, documents, prescription/report URLs, DICOM/PACS URLs, image URLs, insurance member/approval fields and safety answers are sensitive clinical or personal data with no field-level encryption/projection, consent, signed-link, retention or access policy visible in the schema (`115–157,172–189`). State history/reports/approval fields have no immutable actor/reason/source, ordering, CAS or append-only semantics; report status and booking state can diverge (`132–134,167–169`). `allocated_machine_id`, technician/provider/referring-doctor references have no referential or capability constraints (`135,150,153,202`).

The transition map does not itself enforce transitions, concurrency, idempotency, cancellation/refund compensation or role-specific authorization; scan-abort recovery and report publication safety require service-level controls not visible here (`97–109`). `doctor_notified` is a boolean without delivery idempotency or acknowledgement (`201–203`). No TTL/retention/deletion lifecycle, tenant/facility isolation, patient/provider ownership, audit, notification, rate limit, live PACS/object-store validation or migration/index verification is present in this schema. No code was changed and no build/test/application operation was performed during this read.
