# Patient Mobile: Insurance / policy / claim / co-pay — manual semantic review

## Scope and review boundary

This artifact covers the 13 `insurance` inventory candidates. It is a **read-only static client review**. It does not demonstrate provider/payer authorization, policy ownership, eligibility, preauthorization, claim adjudication, payment settlement, ledger posting, booking confirmation, privacy/security of identifiers, or backend state transitions. No runtime/build/deployment or product-source change was performed.

## Required insurance journey boundary

For consultations, labs, radiology and home-care, a valid insurance journey must remain **service/provider/slot selection → insurance request without patient payment → provider/payer decision → authoritative co-pay → patient payment → confirmation**. For pharmacy, it must remain **cart → geographic broadcast → offers with availability/substitution/price/ETA → patient chooses an offer → insurance decision → co-pay or explicit COD/card/cash decision → confirmed order**. The reviewed client sources do not prove these chains.

## Review conclusion

Insurance is not ready to act as a financial or clinical authorization surface. It contains confirmed fabricated policy/limit/benefit values, an unsafe external WebView scraping path that marks data verified, an under-specified claim creator, and legacy co-pay/approval screens that are not bound to a canonical payment or service request. `payment-split.tsx` is a better static pattern but still awaits backend, ledger and runtime reconciliation. All 13 routes are marked only as manually reviewed with findings/contract gaps recorded.
