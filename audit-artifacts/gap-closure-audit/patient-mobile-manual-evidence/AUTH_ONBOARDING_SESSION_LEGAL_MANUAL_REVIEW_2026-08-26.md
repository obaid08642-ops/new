# Patient Mobile: Auth / Onboarding / Session / Legal — manual semantic review

## Scope and review boundary

This artifact records a **read-only manual source review** of the twelve listed Patient Mobile routes and the shared mobile API/session client on the fixed audit baseline. It does **not** prove that the backend accepts, rejects, authorizes, revokes, or securely stores any request; those assertions require controller/DTO/session-store reconciliation and runtime evidence. No product source, runtime test, build, deployment, merge, migration, or live data operation was performed for this wave.

## Required reconciliation boundary

The entries above are route-and-source evidence, not a backend verdict. A future Auth contract reconciliation must trace each CTA to its exact controller, DTO/schema, identity/session service, token store, role enforcement, consent ledger and notifications; then validate unauthenticated, guest, patient, provider, expired-session, denied-permission, error, replay and recovery states. No item in this artifact authorizes a remediation or an assertion of production readiness.

## Review conclusion

The reviewed Auth/Onboarding surface contains several **confirmed client-side defects**: conflicting identifier semantics, a local-only OTP resend control, password-in-route registration state, reset without a propagated proof, a known default guest-conversion password, role-boundary navigation without session action, and local-only permission/completion state. It also exposes session, legal-consent and OAuth decisions that require backend and runtime evidence. The twelve routes are therefore suitable to mark as **manually read with findings/contract gaps recorded**, but they do not close authentication, authorization, legal, privacy, or readiness assurance.
