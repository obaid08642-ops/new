# Patient Web: Consultations, bookings, diagnostics and home-care — manual CTA→contract review

## Scope boundary

This source-only review covers public consultation discovery and slot entry, the unified consultation-booking form/BFF, appointment detail/cancel/reschedule/call-token surfaces, public labs/radiology discovery, diagnostic booking detail, and home-care catalog/detail. It does not validate any upstream controller/service/DTO/schema, slot hold, payment processor, payer, provider decision, real call, diagnostic result, home-care workforce state, or ownership enforcement.

> Required rule for consultation/lab/radiology/home-care/nursing: choose service/provider/slot → cash/card payment **before** confirmation. Insurance requests are sent **without** payment → provider/payer decision → co-pay → patient payment → confirmation. None of those state transitions may be inferred from a page title or a static price/slot badge.
