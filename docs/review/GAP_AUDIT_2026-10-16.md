## 4. Found on production today (signed-out crawl of all 212 static pages, ar/en/ur, 390 px)

- The text checks pass in ar, en and ur: no raw keys, no `undefined`/`NaN`, no crash, no broken image, no horizontal overflow.
- **139 pages show only the sign-in form to a guest.** Most are personal and correct. These must be public or show the public part first:
  - **`/emergency`** (the urgent-help number must never be behind sign-in);
  - `/offers`, `/insurance`, `/insurance/network-providers`, `/home-care`, `/home-care/providers`;
  - `/diagnostics/search`, `/diagnostics/lab-comparison`;
  - `/maternity`, `/mental-health`, `/nutrition`, the `/ai` landing.

  Issue 1550.
- `/offline` and `/settings/connected-apps`: 404.
- `/map` calls `/api/patient/providers/map`, which returns 401 for a guest, so the map is empty.
- Product page: no full-screen zoom, no swipe, alternatives missing, cart button state, slow first visit (issues 1547, 1548).

**Catalogue data** (sample of 423 products from the public API):
- 56% have no `active_ingredient`.
- Where it exists it is not normalized: strength, form, or a whole leaflet with warnings is stored in the field (86 spellings in the sample; paracetamol alone has 12+). This is why "same active ingredient" finds few alternatives.
- Pictures are fine: 55% of products have 2–5 pictures in the database, all on the CDN, and the product page shows them (the earlier sample was wrong; full data audit in `DATA_AUDIT_2026-10-16.md`).

**Security:** the public `GET /medicines/:id/alternatives` returns whole database records: `provenance`, `source`, `source_product_id`, `pending_revision`, `review_reason`, `created_by`, stock counts and more. It needs a public projection like `/public/products`.
