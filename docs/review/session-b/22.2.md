# 22.2 Back-in-stock and price-drop alerts: BLOCKED (owner decision)

**Plan (CHANGED):** price-drop alerts and shared wishlists exclude Rx items.

**What is on `main` (checked 2026-10-18):**
- Wishlist exists (`backend/src/modules/users/users.service.ts`: `getWishlist`, `toggleWishlist`). No shared (public) wishlist link.
- No price-drop or back-in-stock alert model, no price history, no subscription per product.
- Pharmacy has low-stock alerts for pharmacies (`pharmacylowstockalert.repository.ts`), not for patients.

**Why it is blocked:**
1. **Back-in-stock conflicts with the owner decision of 2026-10-06:** pharmacy orders are broadcast and pharmacies answer with offers, there is no stock check. A "back in stock" signal would have no source of truth.
2. A price-drop alert needs a price history per product. `main` keeps only the current price.

**Questions for the owner (answer on this PR):**
- Drop back-in-stock entirely, or define it as "a pharmacy answered an offer for this product"?
- Price-drop: may the team store a price history per product (a new collection), or is the current price enough?
