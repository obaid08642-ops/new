5. The Service is sold between the User and the Provider. The Platform collects the price on the Provider's behalf and issues the invoice on its behalf (Article 7).

1. **The Platform is not for emergencies.** In an emergency call **997** at once.
2. A remote consultation does not replace an in-person examination where the doctor thinks one is needed. The doctor alone decides whether the case suits a remote consultation. Before the first video consultation the User is asked to read and accept the "Consent to Remote Medical Consultation".
3. **A prescription is the doctor's decision alone.** The User may not insist on a prescription or on a particular medicine.
4. The Platform's smart assistant **does not diagnose, prescribe or set doses**. It points the User to the right specialty and shows a medicine's leaflet from the catalogue. It is not a substitute for a doctor or pharmacist.

## 5. Medicines and pharmacies
1. Medicines are shown from the Platform's catalogue.
2. When the User orders, the order is sent to nearby licensed pharmacies and the User picks one of the offers. The chosen pharmacy is the seller and dispenses and delivers the medicine.
3. **Prescription-only medicines:**
   - are dispensed only against a valid prescription, uploaded or issued from a consultation on the Platform;
   - are not covered by any offers, discounts or loyalty points.
4. **Controlled medicines** cannot be ordered through the Platform at all.
5. A medicine's price in a pharmacy's offer does not exceed the price recorded in the Platform's catalogue.
6. **Medicines are not taken back after receipt** unless they are wrong, damaged, defective or expired, as the Saudi Food and Drug Authority's rules provide.

3. **The Platform does not contact insurers or connect to their systems.** Its role is to pass the User's order and the necessary data to the contracted facility the User chooses. The facility asks the insurer for approval through its own systems (such as NPHIES or the insurer's portal), and the order's status is then updated in the app.
4. The insurer's approval and the co-payment are set by the insurer, not by the Platform, and the Platform does not guarantee them. If approval is refused, the User is offered to pay at their own expense or to cancel the order without charge.
