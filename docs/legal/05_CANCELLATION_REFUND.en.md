# Cancellation and Refund Policy

**Version:** 3.0
**Reference:** owner decision 26, and the system's default values.

**Legal basis:** the E-Commerce Law lets a consumer withdraw from a contract within seven days of receiving the product or of the date of the service contract, unless the consumer has already used the product, received the service or benefited from either, and the consumer bears the cost of withdrawal unless agreed otherwise.
- That is why this policy is shown **before payment on every order**.
- The fees in it correspond to a real cost: a booked appointment or a trip made.

**All amounts are refunded to the original payment method,** unless the user chooses the wallet.

## Consultations (in the clinic and remote)
| Case | Amount refunded |
|---|---|
| User cancels 2 hours or more before the appointment | 100% |
| User cancels less than 2 hours before the appointment | 50% |
| User does not attend | 0% |
| Doctor or facility cancels or does not attend, or the call fails for a technical reason | 100% |

**Rescheduling:** once, free of charge, if done 2 hours or more before the appointment.

## Doctor home visits and home nursing
The trip fee is SAR 40 unless a different fee is shown before payment.

| Case | Amount refunded |
|---|---|
| Cancelled before the provider sets off ("on the way") | 100% |
| Cancelled after the provider sets off | 100% minus the trip fee shown before payment |
| The user is not there on arrival | 0% |
| The provider cancels or does not attend | 100% |

## Pharmacy orders
| Case | Amount refunded |
|---|---|
| Cancelled before the courier leaves | 100%, including the delivery fee |
| Cancelled after the courier leaves | 100% minus the delivery fee |
| After receipt | Medicines are taken back only if wrong, damaged, defective or expired. In that case the full amount is refunded and the pharmacy bears the cost |
| The pharmacy cancels | 100% |

## Payment by insurance
If the insurer's approval is refused you are offered to pay at your own expense or to cancel the order without charge. The Platform does not refund amounts you did not pay.

## Timing
- A refund is carried out as soon as it is approved.
- It appears in your account within 5 to 14 working days, depending on the bank.

**Changing the numbers:** the numbers in this policy can be changed by the administration. The numbers shown to the user at payment apply to their order.
