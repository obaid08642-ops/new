# Provider Agreement on the Nabd Medical Platform

**Version:** 3.0
**Effective date:** the day this Agreement is published on the Platform
**Language:** this is the English version. The Arabic text prevails (Article 11.3).

## Parties
- **First party:** Nabd Medical Platform (the "Platform"), whose trade name, commercial registration, VAT number, national address and notices e-mail are as registered with the competent authorities and shown on the Platform.
- **Second party:** the facility or service provider named in the electronic registration form (the "Provider").

## 0. Definitions and attachments
1. The "Service" is the health or pharmacy service the Provider offers through the Platform. The "Patient" is the person who requests it.
2. The following form an integral part of this Agreement:
   - Annexes by provider type (A to D);
   - Annex E, commissions and settlement;
   - the Cancellation and Refund Policy;
   - the Privacy Policy;
   - the Telehealth Consent.
3. If they conflict, the body of the Agreement prevails, then the annex for the Provider's type, then the other attachments.

## 1. Subject matter
1. The Platform lets the Provider list its licensed services, receive orders and bookings, and collect their price on its behalf, in return for a commission.
2. **The Platform is a technical intermediary** between the Provider and the Patient. It does not provide any health service itself, does not interfere with medical or pharmaceutical decisions, and is not the Provider's agent in its professional affairs.
3. The Provider is an independent party. This Agreement creates no employment or partnership.
4. **Staff accounts:** the Provider is responsible for everyone who acts in its name on the Platform, whether employees or practitioners, for keeping login details safe, and for everything done from its account.

## 2. Licensing and eligibility (essential term)
1. The Provider declares that it holds all valid regulatory licences for its activity and will renew them. They include:
   - the facility licence from the Ministry of Health or the competent authority;
   - practitioner licences from the Saudi Commission for Health Specialties;
   - the pharmacy licence under the rules of the Saudi Food and Drug Authority;
   - the licence to provide telehealth, where it applies.
2. The Provider uploads copies of these licences at registration and tells the Platform at once of any suspension, expiry or change.
3. The Platform may verify at any time, and may suspend the account at once if a licence expires or cannot be proven.
4. **Remote consultations** may be provided only by a facility licensed for them, through a licensed practitioner qualified for that type of service. The facility responsible for each consultation is recorded.
5. The Provider supplies the identity and ownership verification documents (know-your-customer) that the Platform asks for, and updates them when they change. The Platform may withhold a transfer, or report it to the competent authorities, where the anti-money-laundering and counter-terrorist-financing rules require it.

## 3. Provider's obligations
1. Provide the Service itself or through its licensed practitioners, according to professional standards and the law.
2. **Full responsibility** for:
   - the medical or pharmaceutical service;
   - diagnosis and prescriptions;
   - dispensing and storing medicines;
   - test results;
   - the safety of procedures;
   - medical documentation.
3. Keep to the prices shown on the Platform and to the medicine price ceiling recorded in the catalogue.
4. Never dispense a prescription-only medicine without a valid prescription, and never sell controlled medicines through the Platform.
5. **Professional insurance:** throughout the term the Provider keeps the professional liability (malpractice) insurance, at the level the law requires for its type of activity. It provides a valid certificate on request and notifies the Platform before it expires or is cancelled.
6. **Advertising and promotion:** any advertisement, offer or promotion of its services through the Platform follows the Ministry of Health's health-advertising rules, including:
   - no advertising of an unlicensed service or practitioner;
   - no publishing of patient testimonials, and no exploiting patients or children in promotion;
   - offers state their amount and duration, and carry any permit that is required;
   - no exaggeration or misleading claims.
   The Platform may remove non-compliant content after notifying the Provider.
7. **No consideration for referrals:** the Provider does not pay or accept any commission, benefit or gift for referring or directing patients, and does not offer one to any practitioner, employee or third party. The Platform's only consideration is the one in Article 5, for the Platform's services.
8. **Dealing with the Patient outside the Platform:** the Provider does not push a Patient to complete a booking or payment outside the Platform to avoid the commission, and does not use the Patient's data for any purpose other than the Service. This does not restrict:
   - continuing the care of a Patient it already follows, or contacting that Patient about the treatment;
   - a practitioner replying to a Patient who contacted them first;
   - the Patient's right to choose their provider.
9. Follow the Platform's cancellation and refund policy and the matching terms of Article 5.
10. Reply to requests and complaints within the periods set in the Platform.
11. Keep Patient data confidential under Article 7.
12. Not use the Platform's name, logo or marks outside the Platform without its written consent.

## 4. Platform's obligations
1. Operate and maintain the Platform, and take reasonable care for its continuity and security.
2. **Make the Provider's dues available for withdrawal** and transfer them under Article 5 and Annex E, after deducting the commission and any amounts refunded to Patients.
3. Provide an account statement and electronic invoices for the commissions.
4. Protect the data of the Provider and the Patients under the law, and comply with Article 7.
5. Notify the Provider of material changes that affect its business under Article 9.

## 5. Fees and settlement
1. **Commission:** a percentage of the value of each completed service. It is the price of the Platform's services (listing, booking, payment, support) and is not a payment for referring patients (Article 3.7). The rate that applies is:
   - the default rate for the type of service in Annex E; or
   - the rate the Platform sets for the Provider, including promotional rates (even zero) for a stated period or a stated number of orders, as shown in the Provider's account and in the statement of each order.
2. **Changing the commission or prices:** the commission and the Platform's fees are raised only on written electronic notice at least **30 days** before the change takes effect. During that period the Provider may:
   - accept the change expressly, or by continuing to use the Platform after it takes effect;
   - or end the Agreement without penalty, completing pending orders on the old terms.
   When a promotional rate ends at its stated date or after its stated number of orders, that is not an increase: the rate returns to the one shown in the Provider's account, and is shown to the Provider before the promotion ends.
3. **Collection:** the Platform collects amounts on the Provider's behalf through a payment gateway licensed by the Saudi Central Bank. The amounts collected are held in trust by the Platform until paid out to the Provider.
4. **The Provider's dues:** what the Provider earns from each completed service is recorded in its account as a pending balance, and becomes available for withdrawal after the settlement period in Annex E.
5. **Withdrawal and transfer:**
   - The Provider requests withdrawal of its available balance from its account once it reaches the minimum in Annex E.
   - The Platform reviews and approves the request, and a large request needs a second approval.
   - After approval the amount is transferred to the bank account (IBAN) registered **in the name of the facility itself** and verified, on the normal bank-transfer cycle.
   - Amounts are never paid to an account in another name. A change of account takes effect only after it is verified.
6. **Invoicing and VAT (the Provider is the supplier):**
   - The Provider supplies the service to the Patient. The service invoice is issued **in the Provider's name and VAT number**. The Platform issues it on the Provider's behalf (third-party invoicing) under this Agreement, and where the rules of the Zakat, Tax and Customs Authority require this Agreement to be registered or approved, both parties complete that.
   - VAT on the service is whatever the law sets for the Provider according to its tax status. The Provider is responsible for it and for filing its returns.
   - The Platform issues the Provider a tax invoice for its commission. The Platform's own VAT applies **only to its commission and to delivery fees it collects for itself** (where it provides the delivery and states the fee before the order), and not to the rest of the value of the service.
   - All of this follows the Authority's electronic invoicing requirements.
7. **Tax and zakat:** the Provider alone is responsible for its own taxes, zakat and fees, and provides its VAT number where registered.
8. **Refunds:** amounts refunded to the Patient because the Provider cancelled, did not attend or made an error are deducted from its dues. In other cases the Cancellation and Refund Policy applies, including no-show and late-cancellation fees.
9. **Payment reversals, holds and negative balances:**
   - If a bank reverses an amount a Patient paid (a payment dispute), the Provider bears it where the cause is its breach or failure in the service. The Platform bears it where it comes from payment fraud and the Provider kept to the Agreement.
   - The Platform may hold the disputed amount only (not all dues) when a complaint, a payment dispute or a refund is pending, or to complete identity verification, until it is decided. The Platform reviews every hold at least once every 30 days and releases it as soon as its cause ends.
   - If refunded or reversed amounts exceed the balance, a negative balance arises. The Platform deducts it from later dues, and the Provider pays any rest within 7 days of the demand.
   - The Provider is notified of any hold, with its reason and amount.
10. **Account statement:** the Provider may object to an account statement within 7 days of its issue, after which the statement is final unless an arithmetic error is shown.

## 6. Quality and rating
1. Patient ratings are shown after completed services.
2. The Platform may suspend visibility or the account if:
   - complaints repeat;
   - quality falls;
   - this Agreement is breached.
3. The Provider is told of any suspension and its reason, and may object within 7 days of the notice. The Platform decides on the objection within 14 days. Immediate suspension is allowed where patient safety is at risk or a licence has ended.
4. **Audit and inspection:** on reasonable notice the Provider lets the Platform verify licences and the records of orders and payments made through the Platform, and cooperates with the competent regulators. This does not include access to a Patient's medical record except as the law allows and with the Patient's consent.

## 7. Data protection and medical records
1. **Roles:**
   - The Provider is the **controller** of the medical record, test results, prescriptions and health information it creates and keeps in its relationship with the Patient.
   - The Platform is the **controller** of user-account, booking, payment, operations and security data it creates for its own purposes.
   - Where the Platform hosts or transmits health content on the Provider's behalf (such as the post-visit chat and results), it is the Provider's **processor**. It processes that content only on the Provider's instructions and under this Agreement, and uses a sub-processor only as far as needed to run the Platform and on obligations no less protective than these.
2. Each party processes data under the Personal Data Protection Law and its implementing regulations, treats health data as sensitive data that needs explicit consent and suitable security measures, and does not transfer data outside the Kingdom except under the statutory conditions.
3. **Use:** Patient data does not belong to the Provider for use outside the Service, and does not belong to the Platform for marketing beyond the Service.
4. **Breach notice:** each party tells the other **without delay and within 24 hours** of learning of any breach affecting the other's personal data or its patients', so that the responsible party can notify the Saudi Data and AI Authority and the affected individuals within the **72 hours** the law sets. They cooperate in the investigation and remedy.
5. **Medical-record retention:** the Provider keeps medical records for the period the health regulations in force require, and lets the Patient see and obtain a copy of their record as the law provides. The Platform keeps booking, payment and invoice records for the statutory period.
6. **Confidentiality:** both parties keep the other's non-public information and Patient data confidential. These duties continue after the Agreement ends for 3 years for commercial information, and without a time limit for Patient data. Disclosure required by law or a court is excepted.
7. The Provider grants the Platform a non-exclusive licence to use its name, logo, photos and service descriptions for display on the Platform during the term.

## 8. Term and termination
1. **Term:** one year, renewing automatically unless a party tells the other at least 30 days before the end that it does not want renewal.
2. **Termination on notice:** either party may end the Agreement on 30 days' notice, completing pending orders.
3. **Immediate termination by the Platform:** the Platform may end the Agreement at once if:
   - a licence expires or is suspended;
   - there is a serious breach, including a breach of Article 3.7;
   - there is fraud;
   - patient safety is harmed.
4. **Immediate termination by the Provider:** the Provider may end it if the Platform seriously breaches its financial obligations and does not cure the breach within 15 days of notice.
5. **Effects of termination:**
   - Pending dues are settled within 30 days, after the commission, refunded amounts and justified holds;
   - the Provider is removed from the listing, and orders already under way are completed;
   - each party gives what is needed for the Patient's continuity of care, and the Provider's account data is exported to it;
   - these continue to apply: confidentiality, data and records, liability and indemnity, governing law, and amounts due.

## 9. Amendment, notices and assignment
1. **Amending the Agreement:** the Platform does not amend this Agreement unilaterally except on written electronic notice at least **30 days** before, stating the amendment and its reason.
   - A material amendment (financial, or one that changes the Provider's duties, liability or rights) needs the Provider's express **new acceptance** in the app, and is issued with a new major version number.
   - A wording, form or new-legal-requirement correction applies on notice without re-acceptance.
   - If the Provider does not accept a material amendment, it may end the Agreement without penalty before it takes effect, and the old terms continue until the notice period ends.
2. **Notices:** notices are given electronically through the Platform and the registered e-mail, and are treated as received when sent to the registered address unless proven otherwise. Each party keeps its details current.
3. **Assignment:** the Provider may not assign the Agreement or its rights without the Platform's written consent, which is not withheld without reasonable cause. The Platform may transfer it to a successor operating the Platform, on notice to the Provider and without prejudice to its rights.

## 10. Liability and indemnity
1. The Provider indemnifies the Platform against any third-party claim arising from its service or its breach of this Agreement or the law.
2. The Platform indemnifies the Provider against any third-party claim arising from its breach of Article 7 or its financial obligations.
3. The Platform's liability to the Provider does not exceed the commissions collected from it in the 3 months before the claim, and does not cover indirect loss or lost profit. This limit does not apply to fraud or gross error, nor to amounts collected on the Provider's behalf.
4. **Force majeure:** neither party is in breach if it is delayed by force majeure beyond its control (such as a public-utility outage, a government decision or a disaster). It tells the other at once and works to resume performance. If the delay passes 30 days, either party may end the Agreement.

## 11. General provisions
1. **Electronic signature:** this Agreement is concluded **electronically** and signed in the name and capacity of the Provider on completing registration. The parties accept its validity and enforceability as if signed by hand under the Electronic Transactions Law. A verifiable PDF copy is kept, showing the version number, the signer's name, the time of signing, the device used and the acceptance record.
2. **Entire agreement:** this Agreement and its attachments are the whole agreement between the parties and replace earlier ones.
3. **Language:** the Arabic text is the reference and prevails over any translation.
4. **Severability:** if a provision is void or unlawful, the rest stays in force and the closest valid provision to the parties' intent replaces it.
5. **No waiver:** a party's delay in exercising a right is not a waiver of it.

## 12. Governing law and jurisdiction
This Agreement is governed by the laws of the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia. The parties try to settle any dispute amicably within 30 days of notice of it. Failing that, the competent courts in the city of Riyadh have jurisdiction.

# Annexes by provider type

## Annex A: Pharmacies
1. A valid **pharmacy licence**, and a licensed pharmacist on duty throughout the hours orders are accepted. Pharmaceutical products are sold through the tracking system approved by the Saudi Food and Drug Authority.
2. **Offers:**
   - They are made on Patients' orders sent to nearby pharmacies.
   - Each medicine line is linked to a catalogue item by search or barcode.
   - The price does not exceed the recorded ceiling.
   - Unlinked lines are reviewed by the administration before confirmation.
3. **Prescription medicines:** dispensed only after the prescription is verified, with no discount or offer. Controlled medicines are not sold through the Platform.
4. **Delivery:** the pharmacy is responsible, itself or through an approved provider, while keeping storage conditions, including refrigeration. A delivery fee the pharmacy sets for itself belongs to it.
5. **Substitution:** any substitute medicine is made only with the Patient's consent through the chat.
6. **Returns:** after receipt, medicines are taken back only if wrong, damaged, defective or expired, and the pharmacy bears the cost in that case.

## Annex B: Doctors and facilities (in-person and remote consultations, home visits)
1. Each doctor practises through a **licensed facility**, and their Saudi Commission for Health Specialties licence number is shown after verification.
2. **Remote consultation:**
   - Provided only by a facility licensed for telehealth, through a doctor qualified for it.
   - Recorded in the facility's medical record, and the Patient's consent is taken before the first one.
   - The doctor refers the Patient for an in-person examination when needed.
3. **Prescriptions** are issued under the law. The doctor may decline to issue one.
4. **Post-visit chat:**
   - Open to the Patient for 72 hours, for text, photos and files, not calls.
   - The doctor closes it or extends it once.
5. **Home visits:** keep the appointment and update the status in the app: "on the way", then "arrived".
6. **Health insurance:** if the Provider offers its services within an insurer's network, it alone is responsible for:
   - checking the Patient's eligibility and any prior approval that is required;
   - submitting claims through the National Platform for Health Information Exchange (NPHIES) as the Council of Health Insurance requires;
   - collecting the Patient's stated co-payment;
   - accepting any rejection or reduction of a claim without recourse to the Platform.
   The Platform only passes on the data that the Provider approves.

## Annex C: Laboratories and imaging centres
1. Facility licence and the accreditation required.
2. **Home sample collection:** by a licensed technician, with a sample chain of custody.
3. **Results:**
   - Uploaded to the Platform as a private document seen only by the Patient and whoever they authorise.
   - Sent to the Patient by notification.

## Annex D: Home nursing
1. Facility licence, and practitioner licences from the Saudi Commission for Health Specialties.
2. **Limits of procedures:** no procedure outside the licence scope, or without a medical order where the law requires one.
3. **During the visit:** keep professional identification, the appointment and infection-control standards, and document the visit.

## Annex E: Commissions and settlement
1. **Default commission by type of service:**

| Type of service | Commission |
|---|---|
| Pharmacies (medicine orders) | 10% |
| Doctors (consultations) | 15% |
| Laboratories | 12% |
| Imaging centres | 12% |
| Home nursing | 15% |

   - These rates apply unless the Platform sets another or a promotional rate for the Provider under Article 5.1. The rate that applies is shown in the Provider's account.
   - The Platform's VAT applies only to its commission and to delivery fees it collects for itself, at the statutory rate (15% when this text was written).
2. **Settlement period:** what the Provider earns from each completed service becomes available for withdrawal 3 days after the service is completed, unless the Platform shows another period for that type of service in the Provider's account.
3. **Minimum withdrawal:** SAR 100. The minimum that applies is shown in the Provider's account.
4. **Objection period for the account statement:** 7 days from its issue, under Article 5.10.
