# Privacy and Personal Data Protection Policy

**Version:** 3.0
**Reference:** the Personal Data Protection Law and its implementing regulations, and the Regulation on the Transfer of Personal Data outside the Kingdom.
**Language:** this is the English version. The Arabic text prevails (Article 13).

## 1. Who we are
- **Controller:** the Nabd Medical Platform, whose commercial registration, VAT number and national address are as registered with the competent authorities and shown on the Platform.
- **Data protection officer:** reachable from the "Privacy and data" section of the settings, or at the e-mail shown on the Platform.
- **Providers:** every Provider that processes a patient's health data to give care is an independent controller of that data and is bound by the law. When the Platform hosts health content for a Provider (such as the post-visit chat and results), the Platform is that Provider's processor and processes it only on its instructions.

## 2. The data we collect

| Category | Examples | Purpose | Legal basis |
|---|---|---|---|
| Identity and contact | Name, phone, e-mail, date of birth, sex | Creating the account and contact | Performing the contract |
| Address and location | National address, delivery or visit location, and geographic location where allowed | Delivery, visits and showing the nearest | Performing the contract; consent for location |
| **Health data (sensitive)** | Prescriptions, reports, conditions and allergies, test results, consultation chats | Giving care through the Provider | **Explicit consent**, and what the law requires |
| Insurance data | Insurer, class, policy number, card image | Passing it to the contracted facility so it can ask the insurer for approval | Performing the contract |
| Payment data | The transaction reference only (we do not store card data) | Payment, refunds and invoicing | Performing the contract; legal obligation |
| Technical data | Device, operating system, security logs, IP address | Security and fraud prevention | Legitimate interest; legal obligation |

## 3. Our principles
1. **We collect the minimum** data needed for each purpose.
2. **We do not sell data** to anyone.
3. **We never use health data for marketing.**
4. Health data is **seen only by:**
   - the patient;
   - the Provider concerned with the order;
   - anyone the patient authorises, such as family members with the permissions the patient sets;
   - authorised staff where operations require it, and every such access is logged.
5. Marketing and promotional notices are **optional** and need consent that can be withdrawn at any time.

## 4. Who we share data with
1. **The Provider you choose:** only what is needed to give the Service.
   - Before an order is accepted: an approximate location, the district and distance only.
   - After acceptance: the full address.
2. **The insurer (through the facility):** when you choose to pay by insurance, we send your data only to the contracted facility, which sends it to the insurer through its own systems. The Platform does not send your data to the insurer directly.
3. **The licensed payment gateway:** to complete payment and refunds.
4. **Technical service providers:** hosting, storage, messaging and e-mail. They work under data-processing contracts, do not use the data for their own purposes, and use no sub-processor without our approval.
5. **Competent government authorities:** where the law requires it.

## 5. Where data is kept, and transfer outside the Kingdom
1. The Platform's data, or its backup copies, may be hosted by providers inside or outside the Kingdom, depending on the Platform's technical set-up at the time.
2. Any transfer or storage of personal data outside the Kingdom happens only:
   - to the extent necessary;
   - after a risk assessment;
   - under one of the safeguards the Regulation on the Transfer of Personal Data outside the Kingdom allows (such as standard contractual clauses), or under a decision of the Saudi Data and AI Authority;
   - with regard to what the Ministry of Health requires for health data.
3. **Images and files:**
   - Product and provider images: public.
   - Health documents and licences: private, shown only after the requester's entitlement is checked.

## 6. How long we keep data
1. **Account data:** for the life of the account, then deleted or anonymised within 30 days of a deletion request.
2. **Medical records:** kept by the Provider for the period the Ministry of Health's rules require.
3. **Invoices and financial records:** kept for the period the Zakat, Tax and Customs Authority's rules require.
4. **Security logs:** 12 months.
5. **Backups:** deleted automatically on their cycle, and kept in a separate encrypted place.

## 7. Your rights
Under the Personal Data Protection Law you have the right to:
1. Know how your data is collected and used; this policy is part of that.
2. See your data and get a copy in a readable form.
3. Correct and update your data.
4. Ask for your data to be destroyed once its purpose has ended, subject to what must be kept by law.
5. Withdraw your consent to processing based on consent, without retroactive effect.

Requests are made from **Settings, Privacy and data**, or by the e-mail shown on the Platform. We reply within 30 days.

## 8. Security
- Encrypted connections.
- Limited permissions for each role.
- Multi-factor authentication for Providers and the administration.
- A log of every access to health records.
- Private encrypted backups.
- Regular security testing.

## 9. Data incidents
When an incident affects personal data we undertake to notify:
- **The competent authority:** within 72 hours of becoming aware, where it may harm the data or its subjects or conflict with their rights and interests.
- **The data subjects:** without undue delay where that is required, under the law and its regulations.

Providers tell us of any incident affecting their patients' data within 24 hours of learning of it, so that we can report within the statutory period.

## 10. Minors
No account is created for anyone under 18 except through their guardian, and a minor's data is managed from the guardian's account.

## 11. Cookies on the website
We use files that are necessary to run the website and keep it secure. Measurement and analytics files are switched on only with your consent, and you can change your choice in the site settings.

## 12. Contact and complaints
- **Contact:** from the "Privacy and data" section of the settings, or by the e-mail and phone shown on the Platform.
- **Complaints:** if your request is not resolved, you have the right to complain to the Saudi Data and AI Authority (SDAIA).

## 13. Updating this policy
We tell you of material changes at least 30 days before they take effect, and ask for your consent where that is required. The Arabic text is the reference and prevails over any translation.
