# Consent to Remote Medical Consultation

**Version:** 2.0
**When it appears:** to the patient before the first remote consultation, and again when a new major version is issued.

I acknowledge and agree to the following:

1. The consultation is given by a licensed doctor through a licensed health facility. **The Nabd Medical Platform is an intermediary platform and does not provide care.**
2. **Limits of a remote consultation:**
   - It may not replace a clinical examination.
   - The doctor may ask for an in-person examination or tests, or decline the consultation if the case is not suitable.
3. **Emergencies:** in cases such as chest pain, difficulty breathing, stroke symptoms or thoughts of self-harm, I call **997** at once and do not rely on a remote consultation.
4. **Accuracy of information:** I give correct and complete information about my condition, medicines and allergies. I am responsible if I do not disclose something.
5. **Documentation:**
   - The consultation is recorded in the medical record of the treating facility.
   - The chat and attachments are kept under the Privacy Policy.
   - **The video call is not recorded** unless I give separate, express consent.
6. I use a private place and a secure connection during the consultation, and I am responsible for who can see my screen.
7. **A prescription** is the doctor's decision alone.
8. I may withdraw this consent at any time. Withdrawing it does not affect what happened before.

By pressing the consent button I confirm that I have read and understood the above. The version number, the time of consent and the device are recorded.
