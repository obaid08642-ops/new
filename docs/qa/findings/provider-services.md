# Provider service options (lab, radiology centre, nurse): findings

Journey: `tools/live/j_provider_service_options.py`, run 2026-10-28 on the local stack: 117 of 129 steps pass; the 12 failing steps are the gaps below. The journey stays ungated until the owner decides; the gaps are product defects, not journey errors.

What the journey does: the admin publishes one test, one scan and one nursing service; two labs, two centres and two nurses register through the wizard with different prices, insurance and home options; a nurse changes its insurance acceptance through the settings change (approved by the admin); the patient reads the lists and the price comparisons, and uses the home and insurance filters.

Setup note: `tools/live/j_onboarding.py` `register_type` gained an optional `step3_extra` argument. The lab and centre screens leave `test_categories` and `equipment_list` to their defaults, so an override of those keys was silently dropped and the new test was never enabled for the lab. The argument adds them to the step-3 body. No other journey changes behaviour.

## What works (for the record)

- Admin publishes a lab test, a scan and a nursing service; the patient sees them in the catalogue.
- Each lab registers with its tests, and each nurse with its service and price; the admin approves each provider.
- The nurse's insurance change is approved (`POST /provider/settings/delta` then the admin approval).
- Lab A's own options (home, insurance) are stored correctly on its profile; the problem is that they are not shown to the patient.

3. Gaps 4, 5, 9 (filters): add the filters to the endpoints, or confirm the app filters on the client. The journey asserts the server filter.
