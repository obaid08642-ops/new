## How to read it

- **Wired to** is `METHOD /path` and the controller `file:line` that handles it, found by the TypeScript-AST inventory (`tools/design/screen-inventory.mjs`), which also follows helpers and services a screen imports.
- **Status** of an audited element: `ok` it calls the real endpoint or reads real local state; `fixed` it was wrong and was corrected; `hidden` it is not drawn because the backend sends no real data for it (nothing is invented); `gap` a known hole, listed under the group.
- Limits, stated plainly: the inventory proves an endpoint exists with that method, not that the request body satisfies its DTO. The body check is `tools/audit/clientbodies.js` + `dtocheck.js` (692 DTO routes, 372 matched by patient and provider client calls, 0 mismatches; bodies built from variables are listed as "verify by hand"). Screens without an element audit show their calls but not each button.
