## Questions for the owner (not changed)
- **Coupons (PT-20): where should they apply?** Only the legacy cart / `orders` path applies a coupon (`orders.service.ts`, `cart.module.ts`). The governed pharmacy order, consultations, labs, radiology and nursing apply none, so a coupon shown anywhere else would be ignored at the charge.
- **Insurance claims (PT-19): who pays a reimbursement?** Approval runs the platform refund (`RefundExecutor`), which pays from the platform and then claws the credited amount back from the provider. If the insurer is the one reimbursing the patient, the platform and provider should not be debited. The current rule is kept; needs a decision.
- **Dispute cap:** `refund_full` without an amount refunds up to the cap (default 2000 SAR) or what was paid, whichever is lower; not "the full booking". Intended?
